## Prompt compartilhado

Edit this approved EME Select website concept into a CLEAN PHOTOGRAPHIC BACKGROUND ASSET for the working website. Preserve the EXACT architecture, landscape, perspective, golden-hour lighting, scale and spatial placement of all buildings/land zones in the reference. Do not redesign the scene, change camera angle, zoom in or rearrange properties. Output one 16:10 landscape photograph, no UI.
REMOVE ALL interface graphics and text: entire top logo/navigation/header/button row, headline, subtitle, search box, filters, links, location text, disclaimer text, all property labels/arrows/plus circles, bottom environment tabs, zoom controls and all other floating symbols. Inpaint the underlying scene seamlessly. The top header band must become a continuation of the same sky/landscape; bottom button area must become landscape. Remove the artificial white washes used to protect the UI and restore natural scenery under them, especially at left and bottom, retaining gentle real atmospheric haze. The final image must reach all four edges naturally with no white frame, no empty panel or detached cutout. KEEP all existing buildings and major landscape geometry precisely in place, with matching materials and light. Add only the background content needed to fill the places covered by UI. Absolutely no new logo, text, watermark, icons, diagram, labels or border. This image will be UNDER independently rendered HTML controls, so zero baked-in interface.

Comercial: preserve the storefront retail gallery in the lower foreground, medium office block at center-right, taller corporate tower at upper-right and the shared green pedestrian plaza. Preserve their approved shapes and relative positions. Remove readable shop signs as well, use blank subtle shop fascias.

Terrenos e Terras: preserve the empty city lot in the lower central foreground with its street front, the empty condominium lot at middle right along the curved street and the expansive farmland farther upper right. Preserve the surroundings, roads, homes, farm and exact shapes of these empty plots. Fill UI-covered regions with realistic connected landscape. The image must remain a clean unlabelled photographic land panorama, no text or pins.

Industrial e Logístico: preserve the big freestanding warehouse lower foreground, the long industrial pavilion middle-right and distant distribution center with truck docks upper-right.
